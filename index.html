IEEE ASYU 2026 Paper & Presentation

A CPI-Weighted Web-Based Price Index for Monitoring Inflation and Cost of Living in Türkiye

Co-authored and presented at IEEE ASYU 2026 (hosted at Özyeğin University). The study introduces a reproducible framework for high-frequency inflation monitoring, leveraging a pipeline that collected over 34 million price records across 568,000+ items spanning retail, supermarkets, home goods, cosmetics, technology, and rental housing. Using endpoint-matched product panels and re-normalized TUIK COICOP expenditure weights, we constructed a web-based CPI index benchmarked against TUIK, ENAG, and ITO, alongside a 16-item TUBER-aligned hunger threshold model compared against TÜRK-İŞ data.
